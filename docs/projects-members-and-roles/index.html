Projects, members and roles

Everything you record belongs to a project. A phone can hold several; the one with the green tick under More → Projects is open. Tap a name to open it. Your Tag Library belongs to the phone, not to any project.

The three-dot button on a row holds Publish to server (before a project is on a server), Members , Edit project , Start next season , Leave project (once on a server) and, last, Delete project . An entry your role does not allow stays in the menu, grayed, with the reason under it, for example Only a project owner can delete this project.

Members is also on the More tab, beside Animals . Anyone on the project can open it; its Your role card says what your role allows and names the owners to ask. Look there when something is refused.

Three rows are worth a second look:

  • A contributor cannot remove their own bad bearing. Record a corrected one over it; taking a bearing out of the analysis is an owner’s call.
  • Any contributor may fix anybody’s truth point, unlike a bearing; see truth points.
  • A viewer can export everything. Roles limit what people change, not what they can copy. Choose the person for what you are willing for them to hold.

Changing a role, removing, putting back

On Members , an owner changes a role with the dropdown on the person’s row (each choice explained, such as Contributor — records their own ) and removes them with the icon at the end.

  • The last owner cannot demote or remove themselves; make someone else an owner first.
  • A removed person moves to Past members with their dates, and their records stay, still under their name.
  • Removing stops their syncing and server access but cannot delete what is already on their phone.
  • A past member can be put straight back from their row: no code, no request.

What the removed person sees: a gray mark on the More tab and a gray door-out on the map, and on Sync Status You're no longer on this project, so it has stopped syncing. Their copy keeps working offline. There is no message or notification.

Leaving a project

Leave project in the row menu. The dialog says you stop syncing, your records stay in the project under your name, and your copy keeps working offline. To come back, ask with the project code or ask an owner to put you back. If the entry is gray, the line under it says why; usually you are the only owner.

Deleting a project

There are two acts, and the first does not do the second.

  • Delete project hides the project and its records on this phone; you can undo it. For an owner of a project on a server, it hides it for every member too. Nothing is removed from the server.
  • Delete from server… destroys it. Owners only; others see Only a project owner can delete this project from the server.

Deleting from the server

It destroys every member’s records in the project: bearings, animals, tags and estimates. The dialog offers Cancel , Delete immediately… and Schedule delete . Schedule delete is the normal choice.

  • Scheduling starts a countdown set by the server (30 days by default), so a phone’s wrong clock cannot shorten it.
  • Every member sees it on their Projects row, and gets Export records in the row menu until the date.
  • The owner can stop it with Cancel scheduled deletion .

Delete immediately… skips the countdown and asks again, with Remove now . Records leave the server at once and its backups within the number of days the dialog gives. There is no countdown and no cancel. If you must tell a landowner or permit office when the data stopped existing, give them the backup date.

Nobody’s phone is emptied: each member keeps their copy, which stops syncing. The sync page shows what they see.

Two deleted sections

At the bottom of Projects :

  • Deleted from your devices, still on the server : projects you deleted in the app. The server still holds their records, hidden from everyone. Each row has Restore and a menu with Delete from server… (or Cancel scheduled deletion ). If the server cannot answer, the heading shows with one sentence saying why and no rows.
  • Deleted here, not yet on the server : a deletion this phone has not sent yet. It goes when there is signal, and UNDO takes it back meanwhile.

Members of a project you delete in the app are told, by name, the next time they sync, and anything they record in it still reaches the server.

Restoring

Restore , then Restore project , brings it back on the server for every member, and cancels any scheduled deletion. Members get it back the next time they open TriTrack with signal. A project already destroyed on the server cannot be restored.
