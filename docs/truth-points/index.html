Truth points

A truth point records where somebody physically found the animal, the tag, or both. It is the yardstick for checking estimates against, so it never feeds an estimate itself: TriTrack keeps it apart from bearings.

Recording one

On the Record tab, pick Truth and the tag. Then two fields:

  • WHAT WAS FOUND : Animal + tag , Animal only or Tag only . As the screen says, Only "animal + tag" checks a bearing estimate of a live tagged animal. A tag on its own may have been shed.
  • PRECISION : a radius in meters, required. SAVE TRUTH POINT stays gray until it is above zero. On a GPS fix it is prefilled from the phone’s accuracy, with Raise or lower it if you know better. You often do: standing over the tag with it in your hand, type over the phone’s ±12 m. For a pin placed on the map nothing is prefilled: Not prefilled for a map pin: a pin is only as precise as your placing of it, and imagery can be off by a few meters.

Notes (optional) — e.g. found in burrow is for what you saw. Preview/Set places the pin somewhere you could see but not stand, which is common here.

Where they show up

  • Map: a small square in the animal’s color, labeled from Truth with the tag’s name and frequency (an estimate’s label starts Estimate ). Tap it for the panel below.
  • Log (More → Log ): a row starting 📍 Truth point · , with what was found, precision, finder and how the position was got.
  • Export: its own file; see below.

The panel on the map

  • What was found and the precision, side by side, because each means little alone.
  • Position: one of GPS fix , Placed by hand on the map , Entered by hand or GPS fix, later moved by hand .
  • Found by: the person who recorded it, not whoever last corrected it.

An older point missing any of these says not recorded rather than going blank, so nobody assumes a GPS fix or an animal-and-tag find. Navigate points the Compass tab at the point, with the distance.

Correcting one

Open it with Edit on the map panel, or from the Log row’s three-dot menu: Edit position / precision / what was found…

  • Position: Move on map… and drag the pin, for a point recorded from a vehicle or across a fence. A moved GPS fix becomes GPS fix, later moved by hand .
  • Precision: if you moved the pin, the dialog warns the old figure was for the old spot.
  • What was found.

Who found it and when cannot be changed. Your name is still recorded against the edit.

Who may change one

Any contributor or owner may correct or delete any truth point, not only their own, because a wrong precision spoils everyone’s accuracy check. A viewer sees Viewers cannot change or delete a truth point — ask an owner to change your role. See roles.

Deleting one

Delete is beside Edit on the panel and in the Log menu. It asks first: Where the animal or tag was actually found cannot be re-measured from your desk. Deleting removes it from accuracy checks, the map, the Log and the CSV export, on every device once they sync. You can undo it straight after. The undo restores the same point, with its finder and history.

What comes out in the export

The CSV export is one zip, and a project with truth points gets a truth_points.csv in it, one row per point. The columns to know:

  • found_kind: animal_and_collar, animal_only or collar_only. Filter on this before computing anything.
  • precision_m: the radius; never empty.
  • position_source: gps_fix, manual_pin, gps_fix_adjusted or manual_entry (empty for points older than the field). It tells measurements from map readings.
  • found_by: the finder’s name. Empty means none was recorded; it is never filled from the last editor.
  • Paired tag columns: the tag’s name and frequency when the point was taken, and now.
