User guide

The guide is written one settled flow at a time. A section appears here once the flow it describes has stopped changing, because a guide that documents an unfinished feature is worse than one that omits it — a reader cannot tell which sentences to trust.

Seven flows are written up so far. Reading order, if you are new: joining a project, then recording a bearing, then sync.

Every picture on this site is generated from the app itself rather than photographed by hand, over invented field data — the collars, the people and the study area are all made up, and none of them is anybody’s real project.

Not written yet, and deliberately: anything specific to Android, and the shape of the viewshed boundary on the map. Those parts of the app are still changing. The map section that has been written covers one settled thing — stretching a bearing out to see where it crosses another — and nothing else about the map.

Truth points used to be on that list and are not any more — recording, listing and correcting them all settled at the end of August 2026, and they have a section of their own.
