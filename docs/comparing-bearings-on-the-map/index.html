Comparing bearings on the map

Bearings from well-separated spots cross where the animal is, but the Map tab draws every ray at the same short length. You can drag a ray out to see the crossing. Nothing on the map says this gesture exists, so this page is where it is written down.

Stretch a bearing

  1. Tap a bearing (anywhere on the ray, or the small circle at its base) to select it.
  2. A round handle appears at its tip. If the tip is off screen, the handle sits at the last point you can reach.
  3. Press and hold the handle and drag along the line: out to lengthen, back to shorten. Sideways movement is ignored, so the heading cannot change.

While you drag, the readout shows Drawn to and the distance. Stretch several bearings and look where their wedges overlap.

A longer ray is a wider one

A bearing’s uncertainty is an angle, and an angle covers more ground the farther out you go. A five-degree cone spans about 35 m at 400 m and close to 900 m at 10 km. That is why the wedge widens as you drag, and why two long wedges crossing at a shallow angle say much less than their center lines suggest. Read the crossing from where the wedges overlap, not where the lines meet.

Lengths are not saved

A drawn length is display only: not saved, exported or synced. To put lengths back, double-tap a tip, or use Reset bearing lengths in Layers & Filters : Press and hold a selected bearing’s tip to drag its length. Lengths are for display only and reset when you leave the map. It is gray when nothing has been dragged.

The rays on the Position tab cannot be dragged: their lengths come from the estimate.

When map tiles stop arriving

Every map in the app, including the Position tab, says New map tiles are not arriving. when the background stops filling in. It does not guess why (no signal and a provider outage look the same from the phone), and it says what still works: Tiles already downloaded still draw, and recording a bearing does not need the map service.

Downloading map tiles for offline use

On wifi, open More → Manage Offline Maps → Manage on map , tap New selection , cover your study area, and tap Download . The app asks you to Keep TriTrack open until it finishes. Each phone keeps its own maps.

If some tiles do not arrive, the card says how many: Retry asks only for the ones still missing — whatever did arrive is already on this device.
