How it works

One day of tracking, in order, with a link at each step to the full guide page. The pictures show invented data.

The night before

Open More → Pre-Trip Checklist . It shows what the phone has ready for working without signal, including whether your study area’s map is downloaded. If not, download it now, on wifi. See downloading map tiles.

Take a bearing

On the Record tab, spin the wheel to the tag you hear, point the antenna, and tap POINT at the peak or hold RANGE while you sweep across it. Choose the quality and save. Position and time are filled in for you, and none of it needs a signal. See recording a bearing.

See where the animal is

Take another bearing from somewhere else. The Position tab estimates the location from the selected bearings, with an ellipse for the uncertainty, and says in words when the geometry is poor. Untick a doubtful bearing to see its effect. SAVE POSITION keeps the estimate. See estimating a position.

The Map tab draws the day’s bearings from everyone on the project, each with a wedge for its uncertainty; drag a ray out to see where it crosses another.

Sync when back in range

With a project server, the app sends your records and collects your teammates' whenever there is a connection; a mark on the More tab shows how it is going. Without one, nothing happens and nothing nags. See sync.

Get the data out

More → Data Export / Backup writes spreadsheets, filtered by tag or date if you like, or a backup of the whole project. See getting your data out.

Trying it

TriTrack is not available to download yet. To learn more or try it on a project, see Support.
