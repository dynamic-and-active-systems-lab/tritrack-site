TriTrack

TriTrack records radio-telemetry bearings in the field and turns them into position estimates. It works with no signal and no server, and syncs to a project your team shares when a connection comes back.

This site is under construction. The user guide is being written a section at a time, starting with the flows that have been walked end to end on hardware.
