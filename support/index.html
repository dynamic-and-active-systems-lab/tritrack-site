Support

The app sends you here from More → App Info → Contact TriTrack support , and whenever it says tell TriTrack support (More → App Info) .

Getting in touch

For a problem with the app or the server, a question about it, or help with a project, write to TriTrack support at michael.shafer@nau.edu.

Please include:

  • the version from App Info (below),
  • the phone model and its operating system,
  • the sentence the app showed, word for word, if it showed one (Sync Status usually has it),
  • what you were doing, what you expected, and what happened instead (a screenshot helps).

Which version are you on?

Open More → App Info . The first line is the version and build number; long-press it to copy it into your report.

Please include it. Several builds are in use at once, and a report that cannot be tied to one gets read against the wrong one. The number is read from the installed build. If the screen says it could not read the version, say how and roughly when you installed the app instead.

Before you write: the guide’s answers

  • Sync shows amber, red or gray: when something goes wrong explains each state and what to do.
  • A record is held, or will not sync: some records not synced, and what a name may contain.
  • Asked to join and nothing happened: when asking to join does not work.
  • A project disappeared: the Trash, and project deleted.
  • The map is blank or tiles stop arriving: when map tiles stop arriving.
  • An estimate carries a caution: the cautions.
