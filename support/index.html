Most of this page is not written yet. It will cover how to report a problem or ask a question, and what the app does and does not do without a connection.

Which version are you on?

Open More → App Info . The first line is the version and build number; long-press it to copy it into your report.

Please include it. Several builds are in use at once, and a report that cannot be tied to one gets read against the wrong one. The number is read from the installed build. If the screen says it could not read the version, say how and roughly when you installed the app instead.

The rest of the screen is the full credit and license text for the map imagery.

Getting in touch

TriTrack is developed in the Dynamic and Active Systems Lab at Northern Arizona University. This site is published from github.com/dynamic-and-active-systems-lab/tritrack-site, and that repository is the way to reach its maintainer: for a bug report, a question about the app, or a map tile provider’s query about the requests the app makes. (The app gives map tile services this site’s address so they can reach a person.)
